Craftsmanship, character and imagination

I am a model and prop maker with a keen affinity for sculpting. I have a lifelong love of stop-motion animation and its associated artistry, alongside interests in history, Tolkienesque fantasy and English folklore. These passions come together in my creations wherever possible.
