Walrus (stop-motion puppet)

The second commission undertaken for Workshed Animation and their upcoming feature film. A silicone skin over a ball and socket armature, painted with pigmented silicone. The puppet features replaceable tusks, moving jaw, front and back flippers as well as articulation within the neck and body.

Role

Client
