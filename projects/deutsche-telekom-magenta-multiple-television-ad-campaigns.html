All projects/Puppet & Model Fabrication

2022

Deutsche Telekom Magenta (multiple television ad campaigns)

View 17 photographs ↓

I sculpted a clay maquette based on a 2D design of ‘The Dude’ and adapted a doll to create the cyclist character. I constructed ball-and-socket armatures, sculpted the hands and feet, then moulded and cast them in silicone before painting. Additionally, I created scale roof tiles for the falling shot and provided puppet maintenance throughout the shoot.

Role
Sculptor, model maker, armature constructor, and on-set puppet maintenance
Client
Deutsche Telekom Magenta.
Skills
sculpting, armature construction, moulding, casting, painting, puppet maintenance
