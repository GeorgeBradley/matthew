1:6 Scale Edwards & Roberts Ladies Writing Desk

View 6 photographs ↓

This is a 1:6 scale model based directly off an antique Edwards and Roberts ladies' writing desk. The legs have been lathed and the barley twists hand-carved into them. The lion head carvings were individually sculpted in clay and cast in resin and the same process for the green man drawer fronts. The edge leaf patterns were carefully hand- carved into the wood itself, as well as tiny handmade functioning dovetail joints in the drawer!

Role
Scale model fabricator and woodworker
Skills
