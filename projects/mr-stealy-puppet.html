Mr Stealy (puppet)

View 6 photographs ↓

Stealy is a puppet I made during university based on the character from the animated series Rick and Morty. Part of the challenge was converting a 2D design into a three-dimensional functioning puppet. This project featured my greatest learning of new disciplines so far, I developed new skills in foam work, pattern making, different varieties of stitching and creating a working hand mechanism. All of this, while adapting Stealy’s eccentric characteristics and movements into puppet form.

Role
Student puppet fabrication project
Skills
puppet fabrication, foam work, pattern making, stitching, mechanism design
