Lothlórien (1:30 scale model)

View 10 photographs ↓

My recreation of Peter Jackson’s Lothlórien as depicted in the 2001 film ‘Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring’ home of Galadriel and the elves. All made from my small student accommodation room during the second lockdown.Began as a 3D Sketch-Up model, every component was worked out and built from scratch. The trees poly-carved, the stairway arches carved from chemi-wood and cast 40+ times, and the stair treads laser cut, amongst other processes

Role
Scale model fabricator and model maker
Skills
scale modeling, 3D modeling, wood carving, laser cutting, moulding, casting
